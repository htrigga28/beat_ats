Product outcome

A job seeker can move from one PDF or DOCX and one target description to a verified ATS-safe Word document without AI silently changing a fact. The redesign preserves the existing privacy and advisory-score model while making every important state visible and recoverable.

Experience implementation

Upload and ingestion

  • Responsive source/job-description split.
  • Accessible dropzone, exact file metadata, replace and clear.
  • Required Gemini consent and separate scanned-PDF consent.
  • Real upload percentage plus streamed parsing, structuring, and validation milestones.

Review and edit

  • Dismissible parser warnings and document-canvas editor.
  • Add, edit, remove, and restore every resume section.
  • Immutable extracted source with visible change markers.
  • Comparison success advances to Tailor; errors remain recoverable.

Tailoring studio

  • Compact advisory comparison over responsive split panes.
  • Work and project bullets, capped at ten per explicit batch.
  • Original wording, two or three proposals, exact keyword highlighting.
  • Only Apply Wording changes resume state; opened panels count as reviewed.

Verified export

  • Complete preview in DOCX section order.
  • Manual and AI change summary against extracted source.
  • Truth and advisory confirmations gate download.
  • One compile-and-download action for tailored_resume.docx.

Acceptance and boundaries

All backend and frontend quality gates remain green at the repository thresholds. Desktop and 390px mobile journeys cover happy paths, recovery, keyboard focus, reduced motion, selection limits, explicit application, and truthful export.

Out of scope: accounts, authentication, saved drafts, browser persistence, dark mode, PDF export, template selection, drag reordering, vendor ATS scores, and hiring guarantees.
