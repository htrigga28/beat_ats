# Resume Tailoring Workflow

- Scope: the complete Streamlit application surface; Operate mode.
- Audience: one privacy-conscious job seeker tailoring a truthful resume to a specific role.
- Task: upload, verify extracted facts, analyze gaps, choose bullet rewrites, and export DOCX.
- Primary action: the one action that advances the current stage; all AI calls stay explicit.
- Proof/content: the candidate's own resume, the pasted JD, structured analysis, and adjacent source/rewrite pairs.
- Constraints: no fabricated claims, no score gamification, no hidden persistence, accessible narrow-screen flow.
- Direction: The Document Examination Desk—paper, ink, thin rules, and verification-green confirmation.
- Memorable moment: a source bullet remains visible while the candidate deliberately accepts one supported alternative.
- Unresolved: none for the private single-user v1.
